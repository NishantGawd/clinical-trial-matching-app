1. Multi-Omic & Clinical Biomarker Normalization (HGVS / VCF Syntax)

Market Gap: Existing tools fail when records use non-standard mutation naming (e.g., p.Val600Glu vs V600E, or T790M vs c.2369C>T).

Feature: Integrate a deterministic mutation normalizer that maps genetic variants to standardized NCBI/ClinVar identifiers before running vector or rule matching.

2. Washout Period & Therapy Window Calculator

Market Gap: Commercial matchers flag a trial as eligible based on diagnosis but miss that the patient completed radiotherapy 2 weeks ago when the protocol requires a 4-week washout window.

Feature: Parse timestamped treatment histories and compute explicit washout feasibility checks (e.g., Eligible starting in 14 days post-washout).

3. Geospatial Burden Index & Site Capacity Matching

Market Gap: Matchers suggest trials 500 miles away without considering accessibility or whether that specific trial site is currently pausing accrual.

Feature: Compute a composite distance-burden score that factors in travel stipends, patient ECOG status, and recruitment status.

4. 1-Page Actionable Clinical Trial Action Letter (CTAL) for Oncologists

Market Gap: Doctors don't read JSON or long conversational summaries.

Feature: Auto-generate a structured, printable 1-page PDF referral note detailing: Patient Baseline, Inclusion Criteria Met, Exclusions Cleared, Genomic Markers, and Trial Coordinator Contact Info.
